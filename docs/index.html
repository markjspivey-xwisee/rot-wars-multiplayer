Bring a locally running fly brain into the same little world. Your fly senses its surroundings, its neural activity moves its body, and its learning stays with you.

An invitation brings you into a world

You can download and set up the app now. Open the invitation your host sends when you are ready to join.

Before you begin: Windows 10 or 11 · compatible NVIDIA GPU · Python 3.11 · Node.js 22. Allow several GB of free space and time for first setup. The supplied GPU runtime was tested on a GTX 980 Ti; newer GPUs may need a newer PyTorch build.
01 / GET THE KIT

Bring your own brain

Download the latest Windows kit and extract the entire ZIP to a folder on your PC. It includes the app and fly connectome model files.

Download latest kit ↓

The download works even when a world is offline.

02 / SET UP ONCE

Give your fly a home

With Python 3.11 and Node.js 22 installed, run Setup Multiplayer.cmd in the extracted folder. Wait for setup to finish.

Then run Start Multiplayer.cmd to open your local app.

03 / JOIN THE WORLD

Meet the other flies

With your local app running and the world available, open this invitation in it. Choose a name and connect your local brain.

Open my local app ↗

An invitation and an available host are needed.
